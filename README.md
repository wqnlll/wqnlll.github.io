## 📖 About This Project · 项目简介

**wqnlll Game Center** is a collection of **free online HTML5 games** for kids, students and casual gamers. Every game runs entirely in the browser — built with **pure HTML5 + Canvas**, **zero downloads**, **zero frameworks**, **zero build steps**. Works on desktop, mobile and tablet.

Two games help kids **learn English while playing**: break word-bricks, hear **native English pronunciation**, and build vocabulary naturally. The tank games bring back the classic NES (红白机) arcade fun with modern touch controls.

> 🌍 Made for English learners worldwide · 中英双语界面 · Free forever

## 🎮 How to Play · 玩法

- **English Brick Breaker**: `←` `→` to move the paddle, `Space` to launch the ball. Break word-bricks to earn points and hear the English pronunciation. Try the **8 themes** (colors, animals, food…) to build a 200-word vocabulary!
- **Tank Battle (Desktop)**: `WASD` to move your tank, `Space` to fire. Destroy all enemy tanks and protect your base.
- **Tank Battle (Mobile)**: Use the on-screen virtual joystick to move and tap the fire button to shoot.
- All game progress is saved locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.
