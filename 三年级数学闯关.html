🎯 核心特色

  • 口算练习：加减乘除混合出题，贴近三年级课程
  • 计时闯关：限时答题，越往后题目越难，挑战反应力
  • 逐关推进：由易到难闯关升级，答对越多得分越高
  • 联机对战：同一 Wi-Fi 下两人实时抢答对战
  • 轻快音乐：八音盒《小星星》背景音乐 + 答对答错音效，可一键开关
  • 即点即玩：手机电脑都能玩，无需下载安装

🕹 玩法说明

  • 打开页面选择关卡即可开始，在输入区作答并「提交」
  • 答对进入下一题；答错会显示正确答案提示
  • 时间条走完或生命值耗尽，本关结束并结算分数
  • 联机对战：电脑双击「启动联机服务器.bat」运行本地服务器，手机连同一 Wi-Fi 打开页面、一人建房间一人输入房间号加入

💡 小贴士

单机模式打开页面直接就能玩，不需要服务器。右上角 🎵/🔊 按钮可开关背景音乐和音效，设置会自动记住，下次打开还是你选的。

🎮 Gameplay Guide

Open the page, pick a level, and answer the mental-math questions in the input box, then hit Submit. A correct answer moves you to the next question; a wrong one shows the right answer so you learn from the mistake. The time bar and your life points decide when a level ends and your score is counted.

Two-player battle: on a computer, double-click the bundled "start-server" file to run the local server, connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi, open the page, and let one player create a room while the other joins with the room number. The first to answer correctly steals the point!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Grade 3 Math Challenge free to play?
Yes — it is completely free with no downloads and no registration. Open the page and play in single-player mode immediately.

Can I play it on my phone?
Yes. The game is fully responsive and works on both mobile and desktop browsers with touch or keyboard input.

How does the two-player battle work?
Both players must be on the same Wi-Fi network. One player creates a room and the other joins with the room number. The PvP mode needs a computer running the bundled local server to host.

What math skills does it practice?
It covers mixed addition, subtraction, multiplication and division problems that follow the 3rd-grade curriculum. Levels start easy and get progressively harder under a time limit.
