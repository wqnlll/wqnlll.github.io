坦克大战

📱 坦克大战 · 手机版

专为手机触屏优化的坦克大战，随时随地掏出手机就能来一局！

🎯 核心特色

  • 虚拟摇杆操控：屏幕底部方向键，直觉式操作，上手零门槛
  • 一键开火：大号开火按钮，战斗中不会误触
  • 经典玩法：保护基地、消灭敌军坦克，还原红白机经典体验
  • 即时反馈：实时分数、生命值、敌军数量一目了然

🕹 玩法说明

  • 屏幕底部方向键（▲◀▶▼）控制坦克移动
  • 点击「开火」按钮发射炮弹
  • 消灭所有敌军坦克并保护基地不被摧毁
  • 基地有 3 点生命值，被击中即损失一点

💡 小贴士

手机版同时支持键盘操作（连接蓝牙键盘时），WASD 移动 + 空格开火。也支持电脑浏览器访问，用键盘玩。

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control my tank on mobile?
Use the on-screen direction pad at the bottom of the screen to move, and tap the large FIRE button to shoot. It is designed so your thumbs never block the action.

Can I play this version on a computer?
Yes. When a keyboard is available (including Bluetooth keyboards on phones), you can also move with WASD and fire with Space.

What is the difference between the mobile and PC versions?
The gameplay is identical — protect your base and destroy all enemy tanks. The mobile version replaces keyboard controls with a virtual joystick and fire button for touch screens.

How many lives do I have?
You start with 3 lives. Your base also has 3 HP points; if enemy bullets destroy it, the game is over.
