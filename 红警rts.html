🕹 玩法说明

  • 开局你有一套基地 + 2 辆采矿车 + 1 辆轻坦，采矿车自己在赚钱，不用管它
  • 用左下角按钮造兵，攒钱顺序建议：轻坦 → 重坦 → 光棱/磁暴
  • 左键拖框框选一片部队；右键点到敌人身上 = 攻击，点到空地 = 移动
  • 把敌方 AI 的基地拆掉就赢；自己的基地被打掉就输
  • 选中单位后按 A 可强制攻击指定位置（拆建筑清矿场很好用）

⚔️ Strategy Guide for New Players

You start with a base, 2 harvester vehicles and 1 light tank. Your harvesters mine gold automatically, so they earn money while you focus on the battle — build a few more to speed up your income.

  • Build order：light tanks first, then heavy tanks, then prism or shock tanks. Attack before the enemy finishes their super weapon.
  • Unit selection：left-drag to box-select a whole group；right-click an enemy to attack, right-click empty ground to move.
  • Winning：destroy the enemy AI's base to win；lose the game if your own base is destroyed.
  • Pro tip：select units and press A to force-attack a location — perfect for demolishing buildings and clearing ore fields.
  • Super weapons：save them for the moment the enemy clumps its army, and they can turn the whole battle around.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this web RTS really free?
Yes — it is 100% free with no downloads, no installs and no registration. Open the page and start playing against the AI immediately.

Is it multiplayer?
The web version is a single-player match against the AI. You play as the blue Allies and fight the red Soviet AI, whose difficulty rises as you keep winning.

Can I play it on my phone?
Yes. The same code base supports keyboard/mouse on desktop and touch controls on mobile. A 3D rendering mode is also available and falls back to 2D on unsupported devices.

How do I command my units?
Left-drag to draw a box and select a group of units, right-click an enemy to attack, and right-click the ground to move. You can also press A to force-attack a location, which is great for demolishing buildings and ore fields.
